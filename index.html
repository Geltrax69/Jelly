How to play

Drag the water to stir waves. Drag the block edges to tilt the whole island. Grab pebbles and coconuts and throw them. Scroll to zoom, drag the sky to orbit.

Joe surfs on his own. Rough water makes him brace. Calm water makes him shaka.
